# Taste

After two waves, I keep an idea when it makes an ordinary control better at its job. I drop it when it only decorates the control or turns a simple gesture into a detour, however original it is.

Motion has to follow the hand. In Reed, the rows bend as you drag one past them and then spring back, and nothing steps or catches along the way. A control that resists the hand or jumps from step to step feels wrong to me even when I like the idea, so a few of the cards I kept are getting smoother motion before they go up here.

I also like dragging things directly. In Louver, you slide along a password and only the characters under your finger open.

So far, transitions that rearrange a layout after a click haven't worked for me, but that's still a hunch.
